# Community garden volunteer plan

[//]: # (ob:3517fea0)
## Saturday schedule

[//]: # (ob:0b451d51)
Run a focused planting day from 9:00 to 12:00 for up to twelve volunteers who
sign up in advance.

[//]: # (ob:d1afd687)
## Materials

[//]: # (ob:c05650dc)
Pre-stage seedlings and provide all shared tools on site so the group can begin
planting immediately.

[//]: # (ob:5782a9e9)
## Success signal

[//]: # (ob:e77d8f45)
Measure planted beds and returning volunteers, not only total volunteer hours.
