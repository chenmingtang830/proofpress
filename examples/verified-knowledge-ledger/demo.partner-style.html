Live LLM agents on Project NANDA's NandaTown — a neutral runtime, not ours. Negotiation should have carried the offer; data facts is the nearest thing to an integrity owner. It crossed as bytes and engaged neither. Four parties could have computed its identity — the issuer, the runtime, each receiver, an auditor afterward. Only the auditor did, from outside, with an analyzer that returned the wrong answer first. Agent identity gives you an accountable party; a knowledge graph gives you a well-modeled domain. Neither answers “what were the terms.”

Cooperative relay, explicit pass-through, one run, one seed. A floor, not general robustness.
