## Launch copy

[//]: # (ob:437324c4)
When one agent hands work to the next, a summary is not enough. The receiving agent needs to know what is current, what evidence supports it, and whether it is safe to reuse. We tested Proofpress’s governed knowledge ledger across seven models and three Harvey LAB-derived multi-step legal handoff tasks. Verified handoffs increased aggregate rubric completion from 89.3% to 93.4%, while observed unsafe propagation in controlled stress pairs fell from 8 events to 0. These are bounded, descriptive results—not an official Harvey benchmark score—but they provide a strong product signal: governed knowledge can make multi-step agent work both more effective and more trustworthy.
