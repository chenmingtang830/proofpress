# RelayBench claim boundaries

[//]: # (ob:6a714dce)
RelayBench is implementation infrastructure beneath Richard Tang's proposed long-horizon Proofpress study. This repository contains no benchmark result.

[//]: # (ob:7a627143)
The intended study may measure final contract-package rubric performance, propagation of superseded or unauthorized negotiation state, recovery and revalidation work, conservative false stops, verification overhead, and captured operational telemetry under a frozen paired design.

[//]: # (ob:190fc2af)
It may not claim that Proofpress improves legal reasoning, legal knowledge, drafting skill, retrieval quality, model intelligence, factual truth, authorship, identity, or external authorization. Proofpress binds and verifies declared artifact, decision, evidence, actor, and version relationships under the selected policy; the receiving agent still performs legal reasoning.

[//]: # (ob:d500afd6)
Clean continuity, evolving negotiation state, and integrity faults are separate tracks. They must not be pooled into one efficacy rate. A carrier-corruption detection result is robustness evidence, not evidence of longitudinal work-product improvement.

[//]: # (ob:4b32ef87)
The local H4 calibration uses only deterministic mocks and synthetic TEST-ONLY fixtures. Its outputs validate mechanics only. They must not enter benchmark denominators, model comparisons, product claims, charts, or publishable summaries. Composed episodes must be described as a Proofpress long-horizon extension using version-pinned Harvey LAB materials, never as official Harvey LAB scores.

[//]: # (ob:9f8d1abd)
Source-integrity boundary: the synthetic releases are not Harvey material; the candidate Harvey files are identified only through the pinned acquisition manifest and are not vendored here.
