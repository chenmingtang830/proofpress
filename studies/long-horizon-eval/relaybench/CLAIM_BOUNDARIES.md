# RelayBench claim boundaries

[//]: # (ob:6a714dce)
RelayBench is the implementation infrastructure for Richard Tang's long-horizon
Proofpress study. The repository now contains composed public-task results, including
seven complete frozen three-task panels and a 12-valid-task DeepSeek breadth expansion.
They are Proofpress experiments over version-pinned LAB materials, not official
Harvey leaderboard results.

[//]: # (ob:7a627143)
The study may report the bounded aggregate: ordinary handoff passed 10,654/11,928
rubric criteria (89.3%) and Proofpress passed 11,141/11,928 (93.4%), a descriptive
difference of +4.1 percentage points across 126 valid paired runs. It may also report
that observed unsafe propagation fell from 8 events to 0 across 63 controlled stress
pairs. The scope—seven models, three legal task families, and the frozen paired
design—must travel with both numbers.

[//]: # (ob:190fc2af)
It may not claim that Proofpress improves legal reasoning, legal knowledge, drafting skill, retrieval quality, model intelligence, factual truth, authorship, identity, or external authorization. Proofpress binds and verifies declared artifact, decision, evidence, actor, and version relationships under the selected policy; the receiving agent still performs legal reasoning.

[//]: # (ob:d500afd6)
Clean continuity, controlled trust stress, model replication, and task breadth remain
separate evidence tiers in analysis. The public quality aggregate pools only matched
rubric denominators from the seven complete frozen panels; the safety count uses only
the corresponding stress pairs. Neither number is an official Harvey score, a
population estimate, or proof of statistical significance.

[//]: # (ob:2c932519)
Cross-model effects remain heterogeneous and are retained by model and task in the
receipts.
Kimi is user-terminated route evidence, not a zero-effect result. Missing provider
usage telemetry does not invalidate a completed quality/safety pair, but it makes
the affected token/cost verification-overhead estimate partial or unavailable.

[//]: # (ob:4b32ef87)
The local H4 calibration uses only deterministic mocks and synthetic TEST-ONLY
fixtures. Its outputs validate mechanics only. They must not enter benchmark
denominators, model comparisons, product claims, charts, or publishable summaries.
Composed episodes must be described as a Proofpress long-horizon extension using
version-pinned Harvey LAB materials, never as official Harvey LAB scores. The
DeepSeek 14-task result must report both all-valid and non-floor sensitivity because
three large gains occur where ordinary handoff scored below 25%.

[//]: # (ob:9f8d1abd)
Source-integrity boundary: the synthetic releases are not Harvey material; the candidate Harvey files are identified only through the pinned acquisition manifest and are not vendored here.
