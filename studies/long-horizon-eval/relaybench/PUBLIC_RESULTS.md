# Governed handoffs improved completion—and stopped observed unsafe propagation

[//]: # (ob:a75a49ef)
This frozen Harvey LAB-derived handoff study tests one product question: when professional work crosses a cold agent boundary, does a verified and governed handoff help the next agent continue safely and completely?

[//]: # (ob:98206673)
**Result frozen:** August 26, 2026.

[//]: # (ob:b6fedb3a)
## Results

[//]: # (ob:2ea84b72)
| Outcome | Ordinary handoff | Proofpress | Observed difference |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Rubric criteria passed | 10,654 / 11,928 (89.3%) | 11,141 / 11,928 (93.4%) | **+487 criteria / +4.1pp** |
| Unsafe propagation in stress pairs | 8 / 63 | 0 / 63 | **8 observed events prevented** |

[//]: # (ob:459c9ee5)
The frozen panel contains **7 models, 3 legal task families, and 126 valid paired runs**. The quality aggregate uses matched rubric denominators from all clean and stress pairs. The safety count uses the 63 controlled stress pairs only.

[//]: # (ob:4a94f863)
## What this establishes

[//]: # (ob:a4478ea1)
The result is a strong product-mechanism signal: evidence-bound, policy-governed handoff can improve completion in this bounded panel while preventing the observed propagation of injected stale, superseded, unsupported, or out-of-scope state.

[//]: # (ob:f67607e0)
It does not establish an official Harvey leaderboard score, population-level causality, statistical significance, or improved legal intelligence. The tasks are Proofpress-composed handoff episodes derived from version-pinned public Harvey LAB Contracts materials. Cross-model and task effects remain heterogeneous.
