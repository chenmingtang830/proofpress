# Decision register

[//]: # (ob:b5aaf542)
## Status convention

[//]: # (ob:98dbebdc)
`Proposed` records a candidate design choice. `Frozen` binds an executed choice to its recorded protocol and receipts. `Superseded` preserves a consequential replaced decision. `Closed` ends a study without erasing its invalid, null, or negative observations.
