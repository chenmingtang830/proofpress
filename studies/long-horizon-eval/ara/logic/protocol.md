# Protocol semantics

[//]: # (ob:afeec1e6)
## Research object

[//]: # (ob:c85350e3)
The research object is a version-bound, admitted negotiation state crossing a genuine cold agent/session boundary. It is not generic agent memory, a document-management interface, or a claim that provenance establishes legal correctness.

[//]: # (ob:e801ed15)
## Intervention and control

[//]: # (ob:cd4ee0d2)
C1 and C2 receive the same substantive contract matter, instructions, permitted tools, budgets, and scoring rubric. C2 additionally receives a Proofpress representation and verification affordance that binds the operative artifact to admitted decisions, supporting materials, actors, and version.

[//]: # (ob:ab7f706b)
The comparison therefore asks whether a receiver can safely continue from the authorized state, not whether more context or better legal knowledge improves performance.

[//]: # (ob:db500b47)
## H4 calibration boundary

[//]: # (ob:0ef3542b)
H4 has four stages and one cold boundary before S3. S1/S2 use one worker; the sender exits before S3; S3/S4 use a fresh worker and workspace. The current Phase Zero fixture checks these mechanics with synthetic inputs only.

[//]: # (ob:6039fa04)
## Tracks and outcome families

[//]: # (ob:103ae58c)
Clean continuity, evolving negotiation state, and integrity faults are separate tracks. The proposed H4 calibration concerns evolving negotiation state; a corrupted carrier is a distinct robustness test and cannot alone support a long-horizon effectiveness claim.

[//]: # (ob:2cacfac7)
Future admitted results must report final legal-work-product quality, unsafe state propagation, recovery/revalidation cost, false stops, and invalid/inconclusive attempts separately. Horizon degradation is unavailable from H4 alone.
