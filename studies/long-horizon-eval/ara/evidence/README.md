## Admission and exclusion

[//]: # (ob:ae8292bd)
Only the seven complete panels named in the receipt manifest enter the 126-pair aggregate. Kimi, Phase Zero test doubles, route calibrations, and invalid attempts remain retained but excluded. Further runs belong to a new preregistered study and cannot silently update this frozen result.

[//]: # (ob:daf78085)
Do not include raw private prompts, transcripts, credentials, or undisclosed Harvey materials in this public artifact. Store only the minimum permitted evidence needed to reproduce the stated claim boundary.

[//]: # (ob:bbb0ada2)
## Verification

[//]: # (ob:556c9a03)
Run `python3 ../src/execution/verify_receipts.py` from this directory, or `python3 src/execution/verify_receipts.py` from the ARA root. The verifier resolves every admitted and secondary result path relative to `FINAL_RESULTS_RECEIPTS.json`, recomputes SHA-256, and fails closed on missing or mismatched files.
