## Visual description

[//]: # (ob:ec0f0e28)
- **Shows**: the frozen PR35 product-study headline, aggregate rubric-completion change, unsafe-propagation change, and model-level paired bars.
- **Demonstrates**: a bounded handoff study reported a consistent model-level quality direction and a complete observed safety separation under its own parity-controlled design.
- **Supports**: C06 and G03 as a source-study contrast, not as evidence that the APEX pilot reproduces the same effect.
