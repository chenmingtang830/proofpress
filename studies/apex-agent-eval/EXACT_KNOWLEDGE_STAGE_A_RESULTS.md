## Decision

[//]: # (ob:0503d4a1)
Stage A passed its structural qualification gates and blocked progression to a paid Stage B executor comparison on substantive readiness. ## Stage B deterministic-closure follow-up

[//]: # (ob:8d7f35fe)
The next no-executor slice moved exactness decisions out of free-form generation where the source already fixed them. Official-authority candidates are now projected deterministically from frozen retrieval receipts and controlled catalog metadata; an independent model call only screens requirement responsiveness and cannot confirm controlling applicability. Numeric construction first inventories receipt-local spans deterministically, then asks the model to select candidate IDs rather than rewrite values. A derivation may bind supporting numeric atoms from other requirement slots only when every variable also declares and matches the source requirement ID. Value-by-period slots require a receipt-bound domain that explicitly enumerates every year; endpoints and phrases such as “each affected year” cannot establish closure.
