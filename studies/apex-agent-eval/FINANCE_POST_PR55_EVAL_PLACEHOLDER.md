## Status

[//]: # (ob:a82c3d85)
Placeholder and preregistration boundary only. This PR is a review surface for the future protocol, not an implementation or execution PR. No Finance task, model route, rubric, treatment denominator, cost estimate, or promotion decision is frozen by this revision. No executor or grader run is authorized by this document.

[//]: # (ob:36e02c85)
## Dependency

[//]: # (ob:6dd3489f)
This follow-up is stacked on PR #55 and begins only after its two Legal evaluations are reported separately:

[//]: # (ob:7c5a23ba)
1. the 12 follow-up-lawyer-ask progressive-disclosure panel over two underlying APEX tasks; and
2. the 12-task artifact-native APEX Legal E2E panel.

[//]: # (ob:326a2405)
The Finance design may adopt only mechanisms that PR #55 actually verifies. Candidate claims, disclosure packets, grader verdicts, benchmark results, and model recommendations have no admission authority.

[//]: # (ob:f68eb32b)
## Proposed Finance question

[//]: # (ob:c92d6e35)
After PR #55 closes its architecture questions, test whether the accepted evidence-first and progressive-disclosure mechanisms improve a fixed executor on frozen APEX Investment Banking tasks relative to a full-data-room or otherwise preregistered baseline, without changing the executor between conditions.

[//]: # (ob:989cda97)
The next protocol must distinguish at least:

[//]: # (ob:cf4c5466)
- upstream construction coverage;
- post-disclosure supported factual coverage and disclosure loss;
- executor workflow-rubric performance;
- native artifact completion for each expected-output type;
- unsupported, citation, authority, calculation, and artifact-integrity errors;
- latency, calls, tokens, provider, fallback, and settled cost; and
- scheduled, scored, excluded, and inconclusive denominators.

[//]: # (ob:26f18ff6)
## Required gates before execution

[//]: # (ob:4b3c5639)
- PR #55 reports which disclosure condition and executor routes are actually qualified; route-inconclusive models are not silently substituted.
- Finance tasks, data-room snapshot, workbook start state, task/output types, rubric, grader, attempts, context limits, and stop rules are frozen before the first scored call.
- Normal and Proofpress arms use the same fixed executor, model settings, pristine task state, and grading protocol.
- Document-creation or document-edit tasks are evaluated through real artifacts rather than answer-only JSON.
- Every model call has a terminal receipt covering requested and resolved model, provider, reasoning, fallback, usage, latency, and cost; incomplete cells fail closed as inconclusive.
- Deterministic integrity and policy gates remain separate from any authorized human admission decision.
- Prior Finance PR #54 and PR #56 results remain historical evidence and are not pooled into the new denominator.
