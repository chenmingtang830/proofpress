Mode: Persuade. Scope: the public proofpress.dev landing page.

Audience: enterprise buyers, operational leaders, and technical evaluators at AI NeoLabs, AI-native professional services, regulated vertical AI companies, and knowledge-intensive R&D teams where consequential work crosses agent, human, customer, or system boundaries and becomes a durable premise. Proofpress is organizational infrastructure for multiplayer agent and human work, not a standalone developer utility.

Job: understand within one viewport that when agent output is effectively infinite, trust becomes the bottleneck—and that Proofpress verifies and governs agent-produced knowledge so other people and agents can rely on it. Do not position it as another memory or context product.

Primary action: The hero's “Join as an early design partner” action anchors to `#partners`, where the commercial invitation links to the public, privacy-bounded Notion intake form. The hero's secondary action lets technical evaluators review the open-source system on GitHub. Navigation includes How it works, For teams, Evidence, Evaluate, and a “Let’s talk” anchor to the partner section. After the partner invitation, a native disclosure, collapsed by default, provides a copyable Python-first evaluation path that installs both the project-level governance skill and the local MCP/CLI. Frame this as evaluation in the organization's environment, not individual developer onboarding. Product use and repository contribution remain separate paths. The final commercial action links to the same intake form to discuss one consequential workflow; managed deployment and self-hosted setup are presented as options to explore with the team.

Proof: current open-source repository; a runnable synthetic local demo; a frozen seven-model Harvey LAB-derived study with 126 paired quality runs and 63 controlled stress pairs for unsafe propagation; and published field writing. The product-section claim specimen is explicitly an illustrative workflow with synthetic evidence, not a customer result. Do not imply partner validation, official Harvey benchmarking, multi-tenant cloud, general efficacy, or universal truth.
