Mode: Operate. Scope: the hosted owner workspace, with a credential-free synthetic visual preview at port 7335 (`cd web/owner && npm run preview:ui`). Canonical design rules: `docs/DESIGN.md`.

Audience and job: the human steward deciding whether a candidate may enter governed knowledge and finding what can currently be relied on. Read a real statement, its use conditions, bound evidence, and recorded human authority. The workspace must distinguish reviewing a candidate from browsing already eligible knowledge.

Direction: Boardroom Clarity with readable claim records. The accepted United Airlines reference contributes clear type hierarchy, quiet gray section headers, comfortable controls, bordered groups, and a reading column beside a summary. Keep white paper, near-black ink, thin rules, DM Sans, Proofpress teal actions, and explicit governance states. Bundle fonts locally. Short state transitions support selection; reduced motion is respected.

Shared visual rules: primary ink #171B21, secondary #4E5864, metadata #606B77, line #DCE1E4, wash #F3F5F6, teal #006B7B, accent-soft #EDF6F7, control border #87919A. Page title 38px/700 (32–34px narrower), section 20–22px, prose 15–16px, metadata 12–13px. Input/select height 48px with 10px radius; panels 14px; dialogs 16px; standard action buttons are pills. One solid primary action per decision group, outlined secondary actions, restrained destructive red. Ordinary panels remain flat; dialogs may use a soft shadow. Shared rules live in `workspace-polish.css`, full Review composition in `review.css`, and primitives in `components/ui/`.

Home combines compact neutral metrics with a non-list claim graph. The graph connects recorded sources to pending claim nodes; approved knowledge stays in Knowledge. Clicking a claim opens full review directly. Follow the supplied MSA ledger reference: warm off-white canvas, fine neutral connections, white nodes, restrained teal for processing and muted red for blocked work. No decorative multicolor metric lines or saturated node fills. Pending work is not duplicated in a list. Narrow screens retain a horizontally scrollable graph. Empty states never fabricate tasks; agent reads do not prove use.

Knowledge: the navigation label is Knowledge while `/ledger` remains stable. Default to a map of the server's current eligible owner projection, not all admitted state labels; keep List as an alternate view. Selecting a graph node highlights its connections and shows a concise preview in the existing right-hand record area, with a quiet text action to open the full claim record. Never add a second graph detail card beside the record. Search statements, applicability, and keywords; filter applicability and sort by proposal date or statement. The full record shows proposer, approval authority (human or Owner policy), May support, and Limits & conditions. Supporting evidence and Admission & history use disclosures. Show missing values honestly. Current owner eligibility does not establish access for every agent: credential-specific checks still apply.

Lineage: View lineage is a secondary action from a current claim record. Inspect bound evidence and recorded connections; related claims are restricted to the current eligible set. At 820px and below, replace the wide graph with vertical evidence, claim, and context nodes while retaining selection, readable text, and the admission boundary.

Review: at 1200px and above, a flexible reading column pairs with a 360px decision summary, within a 1320px surface. The reading column exposes the full claim, evidence/checks/history tabs, and supporting disclosures. The summary uses readable fact rows and a clearly labeled Your decision section. Keep deterministic checks, model advice, and approval authority separate. Both the inspector and full review expose complete recorded Relevant when and Validity conditions. Owner review remains an explicit control; a separate, default-off Owner policy may authorize automatic admission of qualifying new claims workspace-wide. Label policy admissions as system decisions, never human review. Opening or reading a record is not a decision.

Responsive behavior: Home metrics remain compact and action rows stack on small screens. Native buttons retain keyboard access. On desktop, Knowledge's map/list and right-hand preview/record are resizable with a keyboard-operable divider; either side may collapse to a narrow restore rail without discarding its content. At narrower widths, use the existing one-pane record flow, with no drag handle. Preserve close/focus-return behavior.

Full Review columns stack below 1200px. Navigation uses a 228px rail, 192px at medium desktop widths, an 80px icon rail at 900–1050px, and bottom navigation below 900px. Short or narrow Review viewports use decision controls in normal flow. Visible focus, a skip link, and shared accessible disclosures preserve keyboard access. Loading a selection must not show a previous record as actionable; unavailable receipts retain a close/retry path.
