01 / WHY PROOFPRESS

The work does not end when the agent answers.

It begins again when someone else relies on it.

A plausible answer is not the same thing as accepted knowledge. The next person or agent needs the evidence, the current version, the decision, and the authority to travel together—not as a loose trail of chats and logs.

A

Know what can be relied on

Project only conclusions that are current, in scope, and explicitly admitted.

B

Keep the why attached

Bind conclusions to the evidence, versions, lineage, and policy judgments behind them.

C

Make authority explicit

Separate deterministic checks and model recommendations from the human decision to admit.

02 / HOW IT WORKS

From agent work to governed knowledge

A visible path across the trust boundary.

  1. 01

    Select the conclusion

    Capture the claim or decision that may matter beyond the current run.

  2. 02

    Bind its evidence

    Carry forward only what is admitted

    Give the next actor current, scoped context with its receipt and lineage intact.

    Governed context
THE BOUNDARY

Traces, retrieval, deterministic checks, and model recommendations can supply evidence. Only human approval authorizes downstream reuse.
